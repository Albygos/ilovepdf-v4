About i loves pdfs

Your Trusted All-in-One Online Document Utility Portal

Our Mission

At i loves pdfs, our mission is to simplify document management for students, professionals, educators, and businesses worldwide. We believe that essential PDF tools—such as merging, splitting, compressing, converting, rotating, protecting, and signing PDFs—should be completely accessible, 100% free, fast, and easy to use without software downloads or forced subscriptions.

What We Offer

PDF Optimization

Merge multiple PDF documents, split specific page ranges, compress file sizes while preserving visual quality, and reorder pages effortlessly.

Format Converters

Convert Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX), PowerPoint (PPTX), JPG, PNG, WEBP, and HTML code into PDF documents and back.

Security & AI Utilities

Protect files with 256-bit passwords, unlock secured PDFs, watermark documents, execute Optical Character Recognition (OCR), and summarize text with AI.

State-of-the-Art Technology & Privacy

Built with ultra-fast web technologies, micro-service processing engines (PyMuPDF, ReportLab, PDF.js), and end-to-end 256-bit SSL encryption, i loves pdfs guarantees that all uploaded documents are processed securely in memory and automatically destroyed within 15 minutes. We never store, inspect, or retain your files.
