Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to i loves pdfs ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information and document files remain 100% confidential and secure. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website at https://ilovespdfs.in and use our online PDF utility tools.

2. Document Uploads & Automatic File Deletion

Your document privacy is our highest priority:

  • No Permanent Storage: Uploaded PDF files, images, and converted documents are temporarily stored solely for the purpose of executing the requested document transformation (e.g. merging, compressing, converting).
  • Automatic 15-Minute Deletion: All processed files and uploaded source files are automatically and permanently purged from our servers within 15 minutes of inactivity.
  • No File Inspection: We do not inspect, read, copy, index, or share the contents of your uploaded documents.
  • 256-Bit SSL Encryption: All data transmissions between your browser and our server are encrypted using enterprise-grade 256-bit SSL / TLS technology.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners to display advertisements when you visit our website:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Log Data & Analytics

Like most web servers, we collect non-personally identifiable information automatically provided by web browsers and servers, such as IP addresses, browser types, language preferences, referring URLs, date/time stamps, and page access statistics. This information is used strictly to diagnose technical issues, maintain server stability, prevent abuse, and optimize website speed.

5. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Under European GDPR and California CCPA privacy regulations, users have the right to request information regarding data collection, request deletion of personal logs, or opt out of data sharing. Because we do not require user accounts or collect personal profile data, no personal identity profiles are retained on our platforms.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or privacy inquiries regarding this policy, please contact our support team at support@ilovespdfs.in or visit our Contact Us page.
