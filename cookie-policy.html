1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by web servers when you visit a website. They help websites recognize your browser, save your preferences, and optimize user experience.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Technical Cookies: Necessary for core website navigation, tool execution, and session state.
  • Google AdSense Advertising Cookies: Third-party advertising cookies used to deliver relevant ads and analyze ad performance.
  • Analytical Cookies: Aggregated, non-personal metrics used to measure server speed and tool popularity.

3. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You can control, block, or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact certain interactive tool functions.
